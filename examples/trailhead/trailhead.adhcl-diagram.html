Design

design.trailhead

A mobile app that runs on the hiker's device and has no app backend. It includes the offline map, the track recorder, the SOS sender, and the forecast view. The map tile publisher, the weather service, the SMS carrier, the GPS hardware, and the operating system are outside the boundary. SMS delivery by the carrier is outside the boundary; the app is responsible up to the handoff to the operating system.

The four domains

Axiomatic Design, from Nam P. Suh, maps each domain to the next. Each record on the right answers a record on the left. The team splits each FR into child FRs only after it chooses the DP for that FR. This back-and-forth is the zigzag.

The two axioms

1. Independence Axiom

Keep the FRs independent of each other. Each DP must change its own FR and, at most, FRs that you set later. Then you can adjust one outcome without breaking another.

2. Information Axiom

Among the designs that keep the FRs independent, select the design with the highest probability of meeting the requirements. This needs evidence, for example trial results.

How to read a design matrix

Rows are FRs and columns are DPs. Each DP sits in the column under the FR that it controls, so the diagonal pairs each FR with its DP. The shape of the matrix gives the classification.
